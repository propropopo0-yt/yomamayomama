POOPOOMAN 2.1 LITE — PERFORMANCE EDITION

This edition reduces lag by disabling real-time shadows, lowering render resolution, reducing building/window detail, shortening the visible draw distance, and reducing city clutter.

HOW TO PLAY
1. Extract this ZIP.
2. Open POOPOOMAN_2_1_LITE/index.html in Chrome or Edge with internet enabled.
3. Click START GAME.
Controls: WASD move, mouse look, Space jump, Shift sprint/dodge, G glider from Level 4, right-click web-pull, F/left-click attack, E continue.

Health regeneration begins after 5 seconds without taking damage. The game uses browser speech synthesis when available.
This remains a stylized browser prototype, not photorealistic AAA graphics. Three.js is loaded from a CDN and requires internet.
